Privacy

This page covers this training site only — not the Minute app, and not awign.com. A final version will be published after legal review.

What this site does

It teaches people in Japan how to record paid first-person video for the AI and robotics data programme of Awign (Mynavi Group), and gives them the organization code to start recording in the Minute app.

What we collect here

  • What you enter in the sign-up form (join.html): name, name in kana (optional), email, mobile number, prefecture, which smartphone you have, whether you have a head mount, and which agreements you ticked and when. We also record which ad or page brought you (utm parameters, the Meta ad click ID, the referring page) and your browser type.
  • Anonymous usage events. On pages other than the form, we may log simple events such as "training viewed" or "quiz completed". They carry no name, phone or email and aren't linked to you.

Why we use it

  • To check you're eligible (phone and head mount) and send you work updates on LINE
  • To match you when you message us on LINE with your name and email
  • To answer your questions and stop fake sign-ups
  • To count how many people each ad brought in (as totals, not about you personally)

We don't use it for anything else and never sell it.

Where it's kept, and handling outside Japan

Your details are stored in Google Sheets, provided by Google LLC (USA), and are reviewed and managed by the Awign operations team in India (Awign Enterprises Private Limited, Mynavi Group). Data protection rules in India and the USA differ from Japan's. Only the people who need it can access it, and we protect it with appropriate safeguards. Ticking the consent box on the form means you agree to this handling outside Japan.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We delete your sign-up details two years after you leave the programme or last contact us. Before then, you can ask to see, correct, stop using or delete your data by contacting us below; we'll confirm it's you first.

LINE and payments

LINE's own privacy policy applies on our LINE account and OpenChat. Details you give us for payment (name, Minute email, payout account) are used only to verify your work and pay you, and are never sold. Payout details for Japan are coming soon.

The Minute app

Once you install Minute, that app's privacy policy and consent screens govern your recordings and account data. This site is separate from it.

Recordings and other people

Never record faces, ID documents, bank cards, addressed mail, nameplates, screens or personal papers. Only record where everyone present knows and agrees, and only during the task.

We never ask for money

No fee, charge or deposit at any stage. If someone asks you to pay in Awign's name, don't — tell us on LINE.
